3. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

3.1 Cancellation Window

Orders may be cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled delivery time. Cancellations must be made through your account on the website or by contacting us directly.

3.2 Late Cancellations

Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the scheduled delivery time may be subject to a 50% cancellation fee. This covers the cost of ingredients and preparation already incurred by our kitchen partners.

3.3 Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • Order delivered to the correct hotel and room but the customer was not present at the time of delivery
  • Customer provided incorrect delivery location or contact information
  • Customer refused to accept the delivery without valid reason
  • Force majeure events as defined in our Terms of Service

3.4 Quality Issues

If you receive a meal that is incorrect, damaged, or does not meet reasonable quality standards, please contact us within 30 minutes of delivery. We will investigate and, at our discretion, offer a refund or replacement.

3.5 Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed within 5–10 business days to the original payment method. The time for the refund to appear on your account depends on your card issuer's policies.

3.6 Refund Exclusions

We do not offer refunds based on personal taste preferences. Meal selections are made by the customer at the time of ordering.

3.7 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or cancellation, please email us at erdenebolor901@gmail.com with your Delivery ID and the reason for your request. You will find your Delivery ID in the email confirmation you received after placing your order.

3.8 Force Majeure

In the event of force majeure (including but not limited to: event cancellation by organizers, government restrictions, natural disasters, or public health emergencies), we reserve the right to cancel orders and issue full refunds. We will make reasonable efforts to notify affected customers.

We deliver to hotels and designated locations within Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia, during the applicable conference or event period. Delivery is available only within the service area specified on our website.

4.2 Service Hours

We operate breakfast and dinner services during the conference period. Your individual delivery time is estimated at checkout. Actual arrival depends on order volume, route order, and road conditions — delivery times are estimates and not guaranteed.

4.3 Delivery Process

Our delivery personnel will deliver your order to the front desk of your hotel or to your room (depending on hotel policies). Each order is assigned a unique Delivery ID — please reference this ID in any communication with us regarding your delivery.

4.4 Receiving Your Order

Please ensure someone is available to receive the delivery during the scheduled window. If no one is available and the hotel front desk cannot accept the order, the meal may be left in a designated area at our discretion. We are not responsible for meals left unattended.

4.5 Delivery Fees

Delivery fees, if applicable, are displayed at checkout before payment is completed. The fee covers logistics, packaging, and transportation costs.

4.6 Delays

While we strive to deliver within the scheduled window, delays may occur due to traffic, weather, event logistics, or other factors beyond our control.

4.7 Incorrect Delivery

If your order is delivered to the wrong location or contains incorrect items, please contact us immediately at erdenebolor901@gmail.com with your Delivery ID. We will make reasonable efforts to correct the issue.
